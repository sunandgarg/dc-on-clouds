## Production safeguards added

- Every profile now has explicit 6,000 and 9,000-word targets.
- Every profile has a machine-readable list of missing requirements.
- A separate production completion queue records sources, courses, fees, module counts, FAQs, faculty, reviews, placements, contacts and internal entity links.
- All profiles remain pending until a human editor verifies source fidelity and readability.
- Courses remain official-source only. An unverified course list is either removed or explicitly queued for review.
- Fees remain official-source only. Blank fees are acceptable when no official schedule is available.
- Removed 115 legacy fee values or fee rows that lacked official fee verification. The two officially verified fee records were preserved.
- Human reviews and questions are counted only when real approved records exist. They are never generated.
- Replaced Anna University's stale generic NIRF label with official NIRF 2025 category positions: #2 State Public University, #20 University and #9 Innovation.

## Why content was not expanded automatically

Expanding 936 profiles to 6,000 words would require more than 5.6 million words. Writing those modules without institution-specific evidence would repeat generic content and risk false courses, fees, cutoffs, rankings, faculty, recruiters and placement numbers. The completion queue makes each unresolved profile visible and prevents structural cleanup from being confused with editorial completion.
