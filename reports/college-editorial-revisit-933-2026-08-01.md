## Decision

The 933 records are structurally usable but are not editorially complete at the requested 6,000 to 9,000-word standard. Missing institutional facts must not be replaced by generic or invented content. Production now records editorial word count, populated module count, source tier, audit state and audit time separately from structural-clean status.
