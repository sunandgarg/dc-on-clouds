## Editorial requirements still pending

Each profile needs evidence-led research and human editorial review before it can be marked complete. The remaining work includes overview, highlights, admissions, courses, fees, placements, cutoffs, rankings, scholarships, infrastructure, hostel, faculty, FAQs, questions, approved reviews, gallery, news, contact information and internal entity links. Modules without reliable evidence should remain absent rather than being invented.

No automated system can honestly certify that AI-produced text is 80 percent human-written. A human editor must review, revise and approve each profile. Plagiarism and AI-detector percentages are not reliable quality guarantees and have not been fabricated in this audit.

## Source policy

- Courses and fees: official institution source only.
- Ranking tags: official ranking publisher only, including NIRF category and year.
- Other factual modules: official sources first, trusted secondary sources only when clearly attributed.
- External links: nofollow rendering is handled by the frontend.
- Internal links: link only to valid DekhoCampus entities.
- If an official course catalogue cannot be verified, keep the course list blank and show: Check the official college website for current courses and fees.
